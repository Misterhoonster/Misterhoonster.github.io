TAID Code Review, privacy. This is a technical demo. Signing in with Google gives it your Google account id, email, name and picture, used only to keep your reviews separate from other users'. Code you submit and the reviews generated for it are stored so you can revisit them, on a database hosted by Neon in the United States, behind an application on Google Cloud Run in Tokyo. If you choose a cloud model, your code is sent to OpenAI to produce the review; if you choose the on-device model, it never leaves your browser. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train models. Delete any review with the × beside it, or email misterhoonster@gmail.com to remove your account entirely.
